One mandate. One decision. Two ways to win.

From September 2026, every business in France must be able to receive e-invoices. That forced-choice moment is Sage's window to capture the white-space — the question is how we capture it, without fragmenting the offer. Two options are on the table. Review each as a live landing page.

The moment

France is a forced-choice moment

The mandate pushes a large wave of businesses to choose how they become compliant — many of them on legacy, paper, Excel or non-compliant tools today. It opens a short window to capture new relationships before competitors turn compliance demand into installed base.

01

A wave must choose

Every business has to act, and most aren't equipped today — creating fresh demand across the whole market.

02

A short window

New relationships are won now. Competitors are chasing the same pool with aggressive free offers.

03

Free entry removes friction

A paid-only entry adds friction exactly where Sage needs reach and habit formation. Free entry lowers the first barrier.

04

Protect the paid value

Free entry must create natural paths into Sage Active Starter — not permanent low-value usage. Usage and feature boundaries hold the line.

The building blocks

Two products, two mindsets

Both options below use the same two products. The difference is how prominently each is offered.

Sage Accès PA

Compliance bridge

For "keep my software" customers

For businesses that need to comply but aren't ready to change their invoicing or accounting system — and those still on Excel who don't want a professional tool yet. A simple standalone portal to receive, import, convert and transmit compliant invoices through Sage PA, with minimal disruption and a mostly manual workflow. It keeps them compliant today and gives Sage a future migration path.

Sage Active Freemium

Growth platform

For "help me modernise" customers

For businesses on paper, Excel or basic tools where the mandate is a reason to adopt professional software. A no-cost first step into professional cloud accounting — compliant invoicing, basic records and invoice tracking, with a deliberately narrow free tier (5 invoices / month, 1 user) and paid gates around AI, bank feeds, VAT, accounting, reporting and collaboration. Capture the relationship now, convert on usage into Sage Active Starter.

The decision

Two ways to capture the white-space

The choice is between a cleaner platform-led motion and a broader dual proposition that may capture more mindsets but requires sharper orchestration. Review each as a live landing page.

Proposed direction
A

Active-led, Accès PA as fallback

Clean platform bet — default the market to Active.

Lead white-space demand to Sage Active Freemium. Use Accès PA only for customers who cannot or will not move from their current system before the mandate.

How it worksOne campaign defaults to Active. Qualification routes only "keep my software / just compliant" cases to Accès PA as a short-term fallback.
Pros
  • Clear strategic story
  • Concentrates traffic & spend
  • Builds the Active installed base
  • Lower confusion risk
Cons
  • May miss "just compliant" buyers
  • Needs smart qualification
  • Accès PA feels secondary
  • Less visible choice
