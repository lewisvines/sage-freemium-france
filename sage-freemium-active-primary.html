Why now

The reform is on a fixed timeline

France is moving every business to electronic invoicing in phases. One obligation applies to everyone, and it arrives in 2026. Acting early removes the risk of a last-minute scramble.

Sept 2026
All businesses

Reception becomes mandatory

Every business in France must be able to receive electronic invoices through a certified platform — whatever its size.

Sept 2026
Large & mid-sized

Large businesses also issue

Large and intermediate-sized businesses must also issue their invoices electronically from this date.

Sept 2027
Small & micro

Smaller businesses issue

Small and micro-businesses move to mandatory electronic issuing, completing the rollout.

1 September 2026 is the planned application date, with a grace period that may run to December 2026. Dates follow the current reform timetable and DGFiP specifications, which remain subject to confirmation.
